Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This website belongs to two service lines run by the same person. This page explains, in plain language, what information we receive, why we use it and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who is responsible

Darío Reyes, owner of DR Lawn & Landscape Maintenance and Island Pest Control & Garden Care, in Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras. He is responsible for the information you send us. For any privacy question, write to WhatsApp +504 9835-0265.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us when you write on WhatsApp or use the contact form: your name, your phone number (WhatsApp shows it to us), where your property is, the service you need, and anything else you include in your message, such as photos of the problem.

How the contact form works

The form does not send anything to us or to this website. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, your browser opens WhatsApp with your message already written, and you decide whether to send it. Nothing you type in the form is saved or stored on this website.

WhatsApp

Our conversations happen on WhatsApp, a service run by Meta. WhatsApp handles your messages under its own rules: WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

Why we use your information

To answer your questions, prepare quotes, schedule visits, do the work and follow up afterwards. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and job details while we work together and for up to two years after your last service, so we can handle follow-up visits and keep our own records. If you ask, we delete them sooner, unless the law requires us to keep something, such as a record of a payment.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to WhatsApp +504 9835-0265 and we will answer within 30 days. You can also delete our chat from your own WhatsApp at any time.

No cookies, and cookieless visit counts

This website sets no cookies and uses no advertising tags. To learn how many people visit, it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless tool that sets no identifiers, does not track individual visitors and only gives aggregate counts (page views, country and device type). It sets no cookies. Apart from that script, which loads from Cloudflare, the site loads nothing from third parties: fonts and images are served from this website itself, so your visit is not shared with Google, Facebook or anyone else. We checked this on the date shown at the top of this page.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not use that data to identify visitors. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Photos of properties

We only publish photos of a customer's property, on this website or anywhere else, with that customer's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
