Terms of use and service

Last updated: October 22, 2026

These terms cover this website and the services of DR Lawn & Landscape Maintenance and Island Pest Control & Garden Care, both run by Darío Reyes in Roatán. By using the website or booking a service, you accept them.

This website

The website is for information only. It describes our services, but it is not an offer or a contract. We try to keep it accurate, and details such as services and availability can change.

Quotes

Quotes are free. A quote is an estimate and is not binding until we both confirm the work, the price and the date on WhatsApp. If the job turns out to be different from what was described, for example a larger area or a bigger infestation, we tell you before continuing and agree on the new price with you.

Prices and payment

Prices are in lempiras (HNL) or US dollars, as stated in your quote. How and when you pay is also agreed in the quote.

Service area

We work in Roatán and surrounding areas. If you are not sure we reach your area, ask us on WhatsApp.

Scheduling and weather

We agree the date and time with you on WhatsApp. Heavy rain or storms can make outdoor work or treatments unsafe or ineffective; when that happens we reschedule to the next available date at no extra cost. If you need to change a date, please tell us as early as possible.

Pest treatments and safety

Treatments are applied following the instructions on the product label. Before each treatment we give you safety instructions, and you agree to follow them, including:

  • keeping children, pets and anyone not involved away from treated areas for the time we tell you;
  • any preparation we ask for, such as storing food or covering surfaces;
  • telling us beforehand about allergies, pregnancy, pets, fish tanks or vegetable gardens near the area, so we can adapt the treatment.

Your property

Please tell us about anything we should avoid, such as buried irrigation lines, cables or plants you want to keep. If something about our work is not right, tell us on WhatsApp as soon as possible and we will look at it.

Liability

As far as Honduran law allows, our liability for any claim related to a service is limited to the price you paid for that service. We are not responsible for damage caused by safety instructions not being followed, or by events outside our control, such as storms. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Honduras. If there is a disagreement, we will first try to solve it directly with you. If that is not possible, it will go to the competent courts of Roatán, Bay Islands.
