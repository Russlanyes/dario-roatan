About

Meet Darío Reyes

Founder · Agricultural engineer (UNAG)

DR Lawn & Landscape Maintenance and Island Pest Control & Garden Care are both led by Darío Reyes, an agricultural engineer trained at UNAG, Honduras' National University of Agriculture.

That background is the difference: lawn care, garden care and integrated pest management built on knowing how insects, plants and soil work. The goal is safe, efficient methods and long-lasting results, for your home, your business and your garden.

We treat your property like our own.
